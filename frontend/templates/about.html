About the Project

AI that understands sign language

SilentLink empowers users with a reliable ASL recognition interface built for accessibility, speed, and real-world communication.

🎯

Our Mission

To make communication easier for people who use sign language by providing an intelligent, real-time translation platform that works right in the browser.

🔬

What We Build

Real-time hand detection, gesture interpretation, alphabet recognition, phrase signs with bilingual voice output, and app control via gestures.

💙

Why It Matters

Inclusive technology reduces barriers and creates more meaningful connections. SilentLink is built with accessibility at its core.
