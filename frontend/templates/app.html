⚙️ How It Works

MediaPipe Hands tracks 21 hand landmarks locally in your browser. The normalized landmark vector is sent to the SilentLink backend over WebSocket, which runs the recognizer pipeline (phrase rules → MLP alphabet), builds the sentence, and translates to five languages.

✅ Best Results

  • Keep your hand centered in frame
  • Use a plain, well-lit background
  • Hold each sign steady for ~0.5s
  • One hand at a time

🚀 Cursor Control Mode

  • Index tip position → cursor position
  • Index+Thumb pinch → left click
  • Index+Middle + vertical drag → scroll
  • 1/3/4 fingers up → launch apps
