Inside the Pipeline

How SilentLink understands your hands

From a live webcam frame to a translated, spoken sentence in five languages — every step runs locally on your machine, without sending raw video off-device.

  1. 1
    📷

    Camera Capture

    The browser requests webcam access via getUserMedia and renders frames into a hidden <video> element. No frames leave the browser — everything is processed client-side.

  2. 2
    ✋

    MediaPipe Hands

    Google's MediaPipe Hands WASM module detects 21 3D landmarks per hand at ~30 fps. The canvas overlay draws the detected skeleton so you can confirm tracking quality.

  3. 3
    🔗

    WebSocket Stream

    Only the 21×(x,y,z) landmark floats are transmitted — not video frames. The socket uses automatic reconnect with exponential backoff if you briefly lose network.

  4. 4
    🧠

    Recognition Engine

    The backend runs a two-stage recognizer: 25 deterministic phrase rules first, then a pure-NumPy MLP trained on the ASL alphabet for fingerspelled letters.

  5. 5
    ⏳

    Temporal Stabilizer

    A PredictionStabilizer + hold counter + word timeout debounce raw per-frame noise into coherent words and sentences, mirroring how human signers naturally pause.

  6. 6
    🌐

    5-Language Translation

    Phrase signs pull pre-translated strings from the assets dictionary (English, Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French). Fingerspelled words fall back to English with fingerspell markers.

  7. 7
    🔊

    Speech & History

    Click any speak button to hear the phrase through the browser's built-in Web Speech API. Sentences are stored locally in your history for review.

  8. 8
    🖱️

    Computer Control (opt-in)

    Switch to Cursor Control mode to move your mouse with the index tip, click by pinching, scroll with two fingers, and launch apps — all gated behind an explicit mode toggle.
